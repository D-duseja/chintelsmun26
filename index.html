About the Conference

Where Diplomacy Meets Excellence

Organised by The Chintels School, Ratanlal Nagar, Kanpur, the Second Edition of ChintelsMUN is an elite proving ground for aspiring diplomats. More than just a conference — a celebration of diplomacy, critical thinking, and global citizenship.

| EKTA | Unity

Meaningful progress is achieved through collaboration, not confrontation. By fostering dialogue, empathy, and collective action, unity becomes the driving force behind lasting peace and global cooperation.

| AKHANDTA | Integrity

In an era of uncertainty and misinformation, Akhandta stands as the foundation of ethical leadership and principled diplomacy. It inspires nations to uphold accountability and pursue justice with unwavering conviction.

| VIVIDHTA | Diversity

Vividhta reminds us that diversity is our greatest strength. By embracing different cultures, perspectives, and identities, we foster mutual respect, inclusivity, and lasting unity.
